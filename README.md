Olist E-commerce Business Analytics

An end-to-end e-commerce business analytics project using **Excel, SQL, Python, and Power BI** to analyze sales performance, order trends, product categories, and operational outcomes.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Brazilian Olist e-commerce dataset to identify business trends and generate actionable insights from transactional data.

The analysis covers:

- Revenue and sales performance
- Order volume and order status
- Product category performance
- Monthly revenue trends
- Average product price
- Operational and fulfillment patterns
- Business recommendations based on the analysis

## Tools & Technologies

- **Excel** — Data cleaning, merging, PivotTables and exploratory analysis
- **SQL** — Data validation, aggregation and business queries
- **Python** — Pandas-based data analysis and visualization
- **Power BI** — Interactive dashboard and business reporting

## Key Analysis

### Revenue Analysis
Analyzed revenue across product categories to identify high-performing areas and differences in category contribution.

### Order Analysis
Examined order status distribution to understand delivered, shipped, processing, canceled and unavailable orders.

### Time Analysis
Analyzed revenue trends across months to identify changes in sales performance over time.

### Product Category Analysis
Compared category-level revenue to identify categories contributing significantly to overall sales.

##  Business Insights

- `beleza_saude` was the highest-revenue product category in the analysis.
- Delivered orders represented the overwhelming majority of orders.
- Revenue contribution varied considerably across product categories.
- Order status analysis highlights the importance of monitoring cancellations and unavailable orders.
- High-revenue categories can be monitored for inventory, fulfillment and customer-service performance.

## Business Recommendations

1. Monitor canceled and unavailable orders to identify potential operational issues.
2. Track fulfillment and shipping performance to maintain delivery reliability.
3. Focus category-level analysis on high-revenue product segments.
4. Analyze freight and delivery costs alongside product revenue.
5. Use monthly revenue trends to support sales and inventory planning.
